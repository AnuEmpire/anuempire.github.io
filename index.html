WORLD & DUAL-PLANE WARFARE

DOMINION ABOVE THE SANDS AND BENEATH THE EARTH

When the Anunnaki descended upon the fertile crescent, they raised obsidian Ziggurat citadels aligned with celestial meridians. Beneath those monumental temples lies the Kur, a vast subterranean realm rich in ancient energy veins and forgotten vaults.

Victory requires mastering both map layers simultaneously. Secure river basins and trade routes on the sunlit surface while dispatching expeditionary legions into the underworld to claim divine relics and ambush rival capitals from below.

  • Asymmetric Factions & Hero Progression Distinct architectural doctrines, unique unit rosters, and persistent hero skill trees shaped by campaign victories.

  • Surface & Underworld Map Modes Seamless tactical switching between surface empire management and subterranean dungeon conquest.
